# Niwajori Mahjong Privacy

Shared static privacy policy for Niwajori Mahjong (`com.appsmakerdeluxe.niwajori`) and Niwajori Mahjong Demo (`com.appsmakerdeluxe.niwajori.demo`), in German, English, French, and Spanish.

The demo includes optional Google Play Games Services v2 platform authentication and submits completed campaign-level scores to its leaderboard. Before optional authentication, the app performs an on-device age check from a date the user enters; the date is not stored or transmitted, and users can skip it to play offline. Google may process gamer identity, submitted scores, and SDK analytics and diagnostics under its own privacy terms. The game does not retrieve or store player IDs or upload achievements or saves. Local gameplay and saves remain available offline. The full edition does not use PGS.

Review the final APK/AAB permission manifest and SDK behavior before release. This local source is not live until deployed.
