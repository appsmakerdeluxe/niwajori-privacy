# Niwajori Mahjong Privacy

Shared static privacy policy for Niwajori Mahjong (`com.appsmakerdeluxe.niwajori`) and Niwajori Mahjong Demo (`com.appsmakerdeluxe.niwajori.demo`), in German, English, French, and Spanish.

The demo includes Google Play Games Services v2 for automatic platform authentication and status only. Google may process gamer identity plus SDK analytics and diagnostics under its own privacy terms. The game does not retrieve or store player IDs or send scores, achievements, leaderboards, or saves. Local gameplay remains available offline, and saves remain on-device. The full edition does not use PGS.

Review the final APK/AAB permission manifest and SDK behavior before release. This local source is not live until deployed.
